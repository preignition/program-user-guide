# User Account Settings

The **User App** (`/u/`) provides a centralized self-service dashboard for all registered users across the Accessible Data platform. Every signed-in user—whether an organization owner, team collaborator, channel moderator, or survey participant—can manage their identity, security credentials, interface preferences, and notifications here.

---

## 1. Profile Management

The **Profile** tab (`/u/profile`) manages how you appear across teams, discussion channels, and collaborative workspaces:

- **Display Name**: Your full name or professional alias displayed on surveys, team member directories, and discussion comments.
- **Biography**: A short summary of your background, role, or organization (up to 500 characters).
- **Profile Image (Avatar)**: Upload an avatar or profile photo (PNG, JPEG, WebP, up to 5 MB). The uploader allows previewing, replacing, or removing your photo at any time.

---

## 2. Account & Security

The **Account** tab (`/u/account`) manages authentication credentials and security settings:

### Email Address

- View your currently registered email address.
- Click **Update Email** to change your login email. A verification link is dispatched to your new address to confirm ownership before the change takes effect.

### Email Verification Status

- Displays whether your email address is **Verified** or **Pending Verification**.
- If unverified, click **Resend Verification Email** to receive a fresh verification link in your inbox.

### Password Management

- **Change Password**: If you use password authentication, enter your current password and specify a new secure password.
- **Reset Password**: Click **Send Password Reset Link** to receive a password reset link if you forgot your credentials or want to set a password on an email-command account.

---

## 3. Interface & Accessibility Preferences

The **Preferences** tab (`/u/preferences`) customizes your personal application experience across all platform tools:

- **Interface Theme**: Choose between **System Default** (adapts to your operating system), **Light Mode**, **Dark Mode**, or **High Contrast** for enhanced legibility.
- **Language**: Select your preferred language for platform menus, buttons, system alerts, and notification emails.
- **Accessibility Defaults**: Pre-enable accessibility features such as **Read Aloud** audio highlighting or simplified layouts whenever you open compatible platform tools.

---

## 4. Notifications & Subscriptions

The **Subscriptions** tab (`/u/subscriptions`) displays all communication channels, survey notification streams, and announcement lists you are subscribed to:

- **Subscription Directory**: Lists each channel name, application origin, and your active delivery frequency (instant real-time updates vs. weekly digests).
- **Mute / Unmute**: Temporarily pause notifications from a channel without leaving the subscriber list. While muted, broadcasts are held and no emails are dispatched to your inbox. Click **Unmute** to resume delivery.
- **Unsubscribe**: Permanently remove your address from a channel or survey notification list.

---

## 5. Account Deletion (Danger Zone)

At the bottom of the Account tab, the **Delete Account** section provides self-service account termination:

::: danger Permanent Action
Deleting your user account is an irreversible action:

- Your personal profile, avatar, preferences, and notification subscriptions are deleted immediately.
- If you are the sole **Owner** of an organization or team workspace, you must transfer ownership to another team member in the [Customer Portal](../customer/reference/customer/members.md) before your account can be deleted.
- Survey responses submitted anonymously or under past research sessions remain preserved according to each survey's retention policy.
:::
