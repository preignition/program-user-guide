# Real-Time Broadcasts and Weekly Digests

Subscribers choose how much mail the channel sends them. The two modes are **real-time** (one email per broadcast) and **weekly digest** (one summary email a week). The choice belongs to the subscriber; administrators can change it on a subscriber's behalf.

## Real-time delivery

Each approved broadcast is sent to active real-time subscribers as its own email, shortly after it is approved and translated.

Real-time is the right choice for time-sensitive announcements and for people who take part in discussions, because a real-time email can be answered directly. The reply becomes a contribution to the broadcast's thread.

## Weekly digest

Digest subscribers do not receive broadcasts individually. Once a week, the channel compiles the broadcasts sent since the previous digest into a single summary email.

Digest mode suits people who want to stay informed without daily interruptions, and people whose connectivity or data budget makes many separate messages expensive.

The trade-off is responsiveness and participation:

- a digest arrives after the news it contains;
- digest emails are **read-only** — replying to a digest does not start a discussion thread.

A subscriber who wants to take part in discussions should use real-time delivery, or open the web archive and reply from there.

## Switching modes

A subscriber can choose digest mode on the subscription form, and can switch later by completing their account and updating preferences, or by asking the channel administrator. Administrators can change the mode for a single subscriber from the subscriber list.

The change takes effect for subsequent sends. A broadcast already dispatched is not re-sent in the other mode.

## Pausing delivery

Vacation mode pauses delivery without unsubscribing. A subscriber on vacation stays active and keeps their preferences, but receives nothing until the pause is lifted.

Subscribers ask the channel administrator to set vacation mode; administrators can toggle it per subscriber. Unlike unsubscribing, it does not remove the person from the channel's lists permanently.

## What the channel does with the two modes

The channel maintains separate delivery lists for real-time and digest subscribers, so a send goes only to the people who asked for that form of delivery. Subscribers on vacation are skipped.

Imported subscribers start in real-time mode unless an administrator changes them. A channel's first sends after an import reach them individually, which is worth considering before importing a very large audience.

## Choosing a default for your channel

There is no global default that suits every channel. A service announcement channel with occasional, urgent posts works well in real-time. A high-volume community channel often suits digest for most members and real-time for moderators and active contributors.

Tell subscribers what to expect when they join, and keep the digest schedule predictable.
