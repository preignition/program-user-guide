# Tutorial: Take Part as a Subscriber

This tutorial follows a new member from first contact with a channel to taking part in a discussion. It is for subscribers; channel administration is covered in [Take Charge as an Administrator](./for-administrators.md).

## Before you start

You need:

- the channel's email address, or the web address of the channel;
- access to the mailbox you want to subscribe;
- a few minutes to confirm the subscription.

You do not need a password to receive broadcasts. A password is only needed to manage your account in the web application.

## Step 1: Subscribe

Choose the route that fits your situation.

### By email

1. From the address you want to subscribe, send a message to the channel address.
2. Put the subscribe command in the subject line or the first line of the body.
3. Send it.

Commands are accepted in the channel's active languages. The exact words are in [Subscribing and Managing Delivery Preferences](../how-to/subscribing-and-managing-preferences.md).

### On the web

1. Open the channel's web address.
2. Select **Subscribe**.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Choose your preferred language.
5. Leave **Weekly digest** off if you want each broadcast as it is sent; turn it on if you prefer one summary a week.
6. Select **Subscribe**.
7. Open the confirmation email and follow its link.

A web subscription is pending until you confirm. An email subscription is active immediately, because sending from your address already proves you control it.

## Step 2: Complete your account (optional)

The confirmation email and the email subscription reply both contain a link to complete your account.

1. Open the link.
2. Set a password of at least six characters and confirm it.
3. Select **Set Password**.
4. Sign in with your email address and the new password.

Completing the account lets you manage preferences in the app. It is not required to receive broadcasts.

## Step 3: Read your first broadcast

1. Wait for a broadcast to arrive, or open the channel's archive on the web.
2. Select a broadcast to read it in full.
3. In the archive, notice the response count; select the row to read the thread.

If you chose digest mode, the broadcasts arrive together in a weekly summary instead.

## Step 4: Take part

- **Reply** to a broadcast email to add to its thread. Use Reply, not a new message: a new message is treated as a separate submission.
- **Submit a post** from the web application with **New Broadcast**; it goes to the channel's moderation queue.
- **Read the archive** to catch up on announcements you missed.

Digest subscribers cannot reply into a thread. If taking part matters to you, switch to real-time delivery.

## Step 5: Manage your subscription

- **Change language or delivery mode**: complete your account and update preferences, or ask the channel administrator.
- **Pause for a while**: ask the administrator to turn on vacation mode. Do not unsubscribe for a temporary absence.
- **Leave**: use the one-click unsubscribe link in a broadcast, or reply with the unsubscribe command.

## What to expect

- A web subscription needs confirmation; an email subscription does not.
- Broadcasts arrive in your preferred language when the channel has translated them.
- Replies are shared with the channel, not only with the person who sent the broadcast.
- A bounced address stops receiving mail; the channel cannot notify an address that no longer works.
