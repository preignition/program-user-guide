# Blog Application

The **Blog Application** enables organizations and teams to curate, publish, and distribute accessible articles, announcements, and research insights to the wider community.

<!-- @include: ../../parts/in-progress.md -->

## Overview

Articles published through the Blog Application adhere to the platform's core accessibility and presentation standards:

- **Accessible Rich Text:** Formatted with our shared [Rich Text Editor](../../components/md-editor.md), supporting structured headings, image captions, and theme-aware contrast.
- **Channel Integration:** Articles can be organized into channels configured under [Team Settings → Channels](../customer/reference/team/channels.md).
- **Multilingual Support:** Articles can be translated into multiple languages matching your organization's language settings.
