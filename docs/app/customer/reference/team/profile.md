# Team Profile Settings

The **Profile** section manages the primary identity, internal description, and audit metadata for an individual team workspace.

### Administrative Metadata

The top header presents permanent audit trail facts:

- **Created**: Date, time, and user account that provisioned the team workspace.
- **Updated**: Timestamp of the latest configuration modification.
- **Restored**: Timestamp and actor if the team was previously soft-deleted and subsequently restored.

### Profile Fields

- **Edit Button**: Enables editing mode for team administrators to update profile values.
- **Name**: The formal name of the team (e.g., "Customer Experience Research"). Used across the portal, team switcher, and navigation bars.
- **Presentation**: An internal summary field used by organization administrators to document the team's operational scope, department code, or internal notes.
