# Presentation Settings

The **Presentation** section configures the public-facing identity, branding logo, and localized descriptions for a team workspace.

### Branding & Information Fields

- **Title**: The formalized, public-facing name of the team (required, maximum 80 characters).
- **Short Description**: A concise summary of the team's purpose or research mission (maximum 200 characters), used on card summaries and directory listings.
- **Logo Upload**: Drag-and-drop or file selector to attach an official team or departmental logo. The logo is displayed on team portals and respondent-facing survey banners.
- **Description**: A full Markdown-enabled rich text field (maximum 1,000 characters) for detailed organizational background, contact instructions, or disclaimers.

### Multilingual Localization

- **Default Language**: The primary language for the team's presentation profile.
- **Translate Tab**: Allows team administrators to provide localized translations for the Title, Short Description, and Description for any language enabled under [Customer Profile](../customer/profile.md#public-settings).
