# How-to Guides

How-to guides are goal-oriented: each one solves a specific task in the Customer Portal. They assume you can sign in and know which organization you are administering.

## Teams and people

- [How to manage teams](./manage-teams.md): Create a team, switch the active team, pin shortcuts, and soft-delete or restore a team.
- [How to manage account holders and team members](./manage-account-holders-and-team-members.md): Invite people at both layers, assign roles, and follow up on pending invitations.
- [How to manage team audiences](./manage-team-audiences.md): Create reusable recipient lists, import a CSV, and maintain, export or archive them.

## Billing and quota

- [How to manage your subscription and billing](./manage-subscription-and-billing.md): Trials, payment methods, the Stripe Billing Portal, invoices, plan changes and quota.
- [How to buy additional quota](./buy-additional-quota.md): Buy one-off survey or team packs when the plan allowance runs out.

## Branding and configuration

- [How to configure team presentation, communication and channels](./configure-team-presentation-and-communication.md): Public title, description, logo, translations, sender identity, signature, footer and channels.
- [How to create a theme](./create-a-theme.md): Define the colors respondents see across surveys.

## Developer access

- [How to manage API keys](./manage-api-keys.md): Create and revoke team API keys.
- [How to use the interactive API documentation](./using-api-docs.md): Authenticate and test live endpoints.
