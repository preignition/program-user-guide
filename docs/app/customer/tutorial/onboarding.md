# Tutorial: Set Up Your Organization

This tutorial walks a new organization through the Customer Portal onboarding flow and the first configuration steps that follow it. It takes about 15 minutes and ends with a team you can build surveys in.

## Before you start

- You need an email address you can access during the setup: you will verify it.
- Decide on the organization's **name**, default **language** and billing **currency** before you begin. The currency cannot be changed after the account is created.
- Decide where respondent data should live. The **storage location** is permanent once the account is created.
- Have a payment method ready if you intend to continue after the trial. Trials start without payment details.

## Step 1: Create your account

1. Open the Customer Portal and choose to create an account.
2. Enter your email address and a password, or use the sign-in provider offered on the form.
3. Complete any confirmation the sign-in provider asks for.

The page advances to the profile step once the account exists. If account creation fails, the error is shown above the progress stepper; correct it and try again.

## Step 2: Complete your organization profile

1. Enter the organization **Name** (at least three characters). This is the public-facing name of your account.
2. Choose the **Language** used as the default for new surveys, forms and translations.
3. Choose the **Currency** for billing and quota purchases.

The currency is fixed at this point. Choose carefully; support cannot change it later. If you were referred or have a coupon, the code appears in a read-only field.

## Step 3: Choose a storage location

1. Select a **Storage Location**.
2. Multi-region options replicate data across a continent and suit respondents spread across the globe.
3. Regional options keep data in a single country and suit data-residency requirements.
4. Read the confirmation text and continue.

This choice is permanent. It also provisions the organization's database, so it cannot be revisited after the account is created. The [Storage Locations reference](../reference/customer/storage-locations.md) explains the options in detail.

## Step 4: Verify your email

1. Open the verification email sent to the address you signed up with.
2. Follow its link, then return to the portal.

The step completes automatically when the address is verified. Check your spam folder before asking for a new message.

## Step 5: Choose a plan

1. Pick a plan and a billing interval (monthly or yearly).
2. Continue. No payment details are required to start a trial.

The plan sets the survey and team quota. You can change it later through the Stripe Billing Portal; see [How to Manage Your Subscription and Billing](../how-to/manage-subscription-and-billing.md).

## Step 6: Accept the terms

1. Read the Terms of Service.
2. Accept them to create the organization.

If you decline, the portal explains that the application cannot be used without accepting the terms and returns you out of the flow.

## Step 7: Start your first survey

On the success screen you can:

- **Start your First Survey**, which opens the Survey app;
- **Book a Meeting** with the team;
- **Visit the Help Page**, which is this documentation.

## After onboarding: finish setting up

Onboarding creates the organization, its plan and its permanent database. It does not create teams or invite colleagues; do that now.

### Create your first team

1. Open **Customer Portal → Teams**.
2. Select **Add Team**, enter a name and optional description, and confirm.
3. Open the new team to configure it.

Creating a team redeems one unit from the plan's team quota. See [How to Manage Teams](../how-to/manage-teams.md).

### Confirm the organization languages

Open **Profile → Public** and review **Languages**. Enable every language your teams may translate surveys into; a language that is not enabled here cannot be used for a survey or a Listserv channel.

### Add your colleagues

1. Open **Account Holders** and add the people who should manage billing, teams and apps. Account holders do **not** get survey access.
2. Open **Teams**, select the team, then **Team Members** and add the people who will build or run surveys.
3. Assign roles deliberately: Owner and Admin can manage the workspace, Editor can work on content, Guest can view.

See [How to Manage Account Holders and Team Members](../how-to/manage-account-holders-and-team-members.md).

### Brand the team

1. Open the team's **Presentation** settings.
2. Set the title, short description, logo and full description.
3. Open **Communication** to set the sender address, reply-to inbox, signature and footer used in survey emails.

See [How to Configure Team Presentation, Communication and Channels](../how-to/configure-team-presentation-and-communication.md).

### Create more teams

Open **Customer Portal → Teams** and select **Add Team**. Each team redeems one team unit from your quota. See [How to Manage Teams](../how-to/manage-teams.md).

### Set up your theme

Open **Theme** to define the colors respondents see. See [How to Create a Theme](../how-to/create-a-theme.md).

## What you have now

- A customer account with a permanent storage location and a plan.
- One or more teams, with their own presentation and email identity.
- Colleagues invited at the account and team layers.
- Access to the Survey app, where you can create your first survey.
