# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is the purpose of this discussion app?

The Discussion App provides a platform for Accessible Surveys users, customers, and respondents to engage in meaningful conversations on various topics. It allows participants to create discussions, post comments, ask questions, and collaborate with other members.

### What is a channel?

A channel is a workspace for related discussions. Channels are created by teams within the Customer Portal and managed by channel owners and editors.

### Which types of channels exist?

Channels are either generic (open for general community topics and feedback) or specific to a survey. Survey channels are created by survey administrators to gather qualitative feedback, answer questions from respondents, and resolve difficulties.

### How do I create a new discussion?

To create a new discussion, navigate to the channel homepage and click **New Discussion**. Enter a title and description, select an appropriate category (such as *Questions*, *Ideas*, or *Feedback*), and submit the post.

### Can I edit or delete my discussions or comments?

Yes. Authors can edit or delete their own discussions and comments using the action menu in the top-right corner of their post.

### Why can't I create discussions or post replies?

Only authorized participants can post. On the default channel, all team members can create discussions, and all users with a verified email address can join the conversation. Any signed-in user can react to comments and vote.

### How can I report inappropriate content?

If you encounter content that violates our [Code of Conduct](./codeOfConduct.md), click the **Report** button next to the content. This notifies the channel owner and moderators to investigate and take corrective action.

## Notifications

### How do I receive notifications for new discussions or comments?

By default, you receive email notifications for new activity on discussions you have created or commented on. You can adjust your notification frequency or mute specific channels in [User Account Settings](../user/index.md#4-notifications-subscriptions).

### Can I turn off notifications?

Yes. Open [User Account Settings → Subscriptions](../user/index.md#4-notifications-subscriptions) to mute specific discussion streams or unsubscribe from notification digests.

## Privacy and Security

### Is my personal information secure?

We adhere to strict data protection standards. Your email address is never shared publicly in discussion threads, and avatar and biography displays can be updated in [User Account Settings](../user/index.md#1-profile-management).

### Can I make my discussions or comments private?

Visibility is determined by the channel's privacy setting. Channels have two levels of privacy:

- **Public (default)**: The channel and its discussions are visible to all community members. Anyone with a verified email address can participate.
- **Private**: Access is restricted exclusively to invited team members and participants.

## Support and Feedback

### How can I contact the support team?

If you have questions, difficulties, or technical feedback, contact our support team at `contact@accessiblesurveys.com`.

### Can I suggest new features or improvements?

Yes. You can submit ideas, feature requests, and feedback directly within the **Feedback** category of the main discussion channel.
