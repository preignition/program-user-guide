# Discussion App

The **Discussion App** provides an interactive, accessible community forum for team collaboration, customer feedback, and survey respondent engagement.

## Overview

The Discussion App organizes conversations into structured, accessible tiers:

1. **Channels:** Workspaces created by teams to hold discussions around specific topics, projects, or surveys. Channels can be **Public** (open to all community members) or **Private** (restricted to invited team members).
2. **Discussions:** Individual conversation threads created within a category (e.g. *General*, *Questions*, *Ideas*, *Feedback*, or *Announcements*).
3. **Comments & Replies:** Participants can post responses, mark accepted answers on question threads, upvote helpful comments, and react to contributions.
