# Training Course Session 4: Testing and Sharing Your Survey

## Overview

You have built your survey, applied accessibility options, and translated it. Now, you will learn how to test it and share it with your respondents.

*Estimated time: 15 minutes*

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this session, you will be able to:

- Activate your survey.
- Publish a new version of your survey.
- Take a survey to production so its production link goes live.
- Generate different types of survey links.
- Configure respondent account requirements.
- Set up redirect destinations for completed submissions.
- Grant survey access to members of your team.

## Written Steps

Use these steps with the video or on their own.

1. Open **Share → Status** and choose how the survey opens: immediately, manually, or on a schedule.
2. Open **Share → Publish** and create a new version with a descriptive label. Review the build report and resolve any warnings.
3. Open **Share → Distribute** and select **Go to Production**. This redeems one survey from the plan quota and reveals the production link.
4. In the Link Builder, choose test or production, preselect a language or accessibility modes if needed, and copy the link.
5. Decide the **Account Type**: anonymous, personal or verified. Use an invitation run if only named recipients should answer.
6. Configure **Redirection** if respondents should land somewhere after submitting.
7. Open **Share → Access** and grant your colleagues the role they need.
8. Open the production link in a private window and complete a full test submission before sharing it widely.
