# Training Course Session 5: Analyzing Responses and Exporting Data

## Overview

In the previous sessions, you built, accessible-enabled, translated, and distributed your survey. This final session focuses on analyzing the collected responses and exporting your dataset.

*Estimated time: 30 minutes*

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this session, you will be able to:

- Filter your dataset using the Analytics dashboard.
- Export your survey data in different formats.
- Access online help resources.
- Contact support for further assistance.

## Written Steps

Use these steps with the video or on their own.

1. Open **Analyze → Survey** for the training survey.
2. Check the response counter and the summary cards; confirm the number of responses you expect.
3. Filter by a metadata field (for example language or submission date) and watch every card recalculate.
4. Open a question card, then cross-filter by selecting a bar or category.
5. Open **Behavior** if behavioral tracking was enabled, and review the KPI strip and drop-off.
6. Open **Progress** if the survey used invitations, and read the funnel and recipient statuses.
7. Select **Export**, acknowledge the privacy notice, choose the dataset and whether to use raw or humanized labels, then download.
8. Turn on **Advanced** to see statistical summaries and additional metadata fields.

::: warning The video predates the v2.4.0 Analytics redesign
The recording shows the earlier Analytics interface. In v2.4.0 the workbench was reorganized into **Survey**, **Behavior** and **Progress** tabs, cards gained cross-filtering and export controls, and Advanced mode added statistical summaries. Use the written steps and the [Survey Analytics Reference](../reference/analyze/survey.md) where the video differs.
:::
