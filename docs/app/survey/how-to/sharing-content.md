# Sharing Content Across Forms and Surveys

Accessible Surveys provides several powerful mechanisms to share and reuse components across questions, sections, forms, and surveys. Reusing content ensures consistent presentation, preserves rich accessibility assets (such as Sign Language videos, Easy Read images, and translations), and reduces respondent fatigue.

## Reusable Components

* **[Sharing Sections](./how-to-share-section.md):** Publish entire questionnaire sections (e.g. demographic modules or standardized assessments like the Washington Group Questions on Disability) to the organizational library and embed them as live references or copies.
* **[Sharing Options](./sharing-options.md):** Create shared answer choice lists (e.g. Likert scales, country lists, or frequency options) and apply them across multiple questions.
* **[Sharing Logical Expressions](./sharing-logical-expressions.md):** Define reusable visibility rules and conditional logic across multiple survey items.
