## Search

The search functionality in our documentation is designed to help you quickly find the information you need. Whether you're looking for specific topics, troubleshooting tips, or detailed explanations, our search feature is your go-to tool for navigating our resources.

### How to Use Search

To use the search feature, click the search icon (a magnifying glass) at the top of the documentation page. This opens a search bar where you can type your query. The keyboard shortcut to open the search bar is `Ctrl + K` (or `Cmd + K` on Mac).

## Ask AI

If you can't find what you're looking for, or if you have specific questions about using any Accessible Data application, you can ask our AI assistant for help. The AI assistant provides quick and accurate answers based on the content of our documentation.

### How to Ask the AI Assistant

To ask the AI assistant a question, click the **Ask AI** button in the search bar. This opens a chat interface where you can type your question and receive an answer grounded in our documentation.
