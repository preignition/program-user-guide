# Follow-up Emails

**Date:** 2026-10-19
**Version:** 1.0.0

## Overview

A survey can now contact the respondents who asked to hear from it after the data has been analysed: to share the results and reports, or to invite them to a later survey. The editor writes the email on the **Email** page and sends it by hand, to the respondents who opted in for that purpose. The privacy policy names both uses.

## What's New?

### Two new email purposes

* **What it is:** **Results and reports** and **Follow-up survey** sit beside **Reminders** in the email purposes. A field that carries one asks the respondent to opt in before anything is sent; a field may carry both, and then shows a checkbox for each.
* **Why it matters:** the address stays operational — outside the datasets and every export — and only serves the emails the respondent agreed to.
* **Read more:** [Email Reference](../app/survey/reference/share/email/index.md)

### Writing and sending a follow-up

* **What it is:** the Email page has a **Follow-up Emails** section with a subject and body per survey language for each purpose, and a **Send** action. Sending tells you how many respondents will receive the email before you confirm, then emails the respondents who opted in for that purpose.
* **How to use:** write the email; a purpose with no content is not offered to respondents, so no consent is collected for an email the survey cannot send. Write any link — to a report or to the follow-up survey — in the body.
* **Read more:** [Follow-up Emails](../app/survey/reference/share/email/index.md#follow-up-emails)

### Consent records cover every purpose

* **What it is:** one record per respondent carries the purposes they agreed to, so withdrawing one purpose leaves the others; the unsubscribe link on any email stops them all. Retention is the same bounded period (180 days by default, 1–365), and purging the survey's collected data removes the records.
* **Read more:** [Data Privacy and Security](../app/survey/explanation/data-privacy-and-security.md)

## Notes

* The privacy policy now names the follow-up emails and states that a consent record covers them: what it holds, its lifetime, the one-click unsubscribe and the purge audit entry.
* The privacy version bumped with the survey-email privacy release covers this text; respondents are asked to consent again on their next visit.
