# Privacy: Survey Emails and Reminder Records

**Date:** 2026-10-19
**Version:** 1.0.0

## Overview

The privacy policy now names the emails a survey may send, the records reminders keep and how long they last, and the Purge data action. Respondents are asked to consent to the updated policy on their next visit.

## What's New?

### Survey emails in the policy

* **What it is:** the policy states that a survey may email a confirmation or, with an explicit opt-in, reminders; that these addresses are never used for marketing or to identify a respondent; and that they stay out of every dataset unless the editor makes the address an answer.
* **Why it matters:** the platform collects operational addresses, and the policy said nothing about them.
* **Read more:** [Email Reference](../app/survey/reference/share/email/index.md)

### Reminder records: retention, unsubscribing and purge

* **What it is:** the policy states what a reminder consent holds, that it is kept for a limited period and then deleted with the address, that the unsubscribe link works without an account, and that purging a survey's data deletes the records — with an audit entry naming who purged and when.
* **How to use:** nothing to configure; the [Email reference](../app/survey/reference/share/email/index.md) documents the editor side, and [Purge the data a survey collected](../app/survey/how-to/purging-collected-data.md) the purge.
* **Read more:** [Data Privacy and Security](../app/survey/explanation/data-privacy-and-security.md)

## Notes

* The privacy version is bumped with this release, so respondents are asked to consent again on their next visit.
* The account-email line is corrected: the address belongs to the account (held by the authentication service), and a survey holds it as an answer only when a field collects it.
* The policy's new sections are written in English; the existing translations keep their current text until they are re-translated.
